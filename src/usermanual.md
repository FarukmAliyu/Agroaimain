# Crop Disease Detection System - User Manual

## 1. Login
- Open the app at `http://localhost:3000`
- Enter your email and password.
- Demo credentials:
  - Email: `user@example.com`
  - Password: `password123`
- Click **Login**.

## 2. Upload Leaf Image
- After login, click **Choose File** or **Upload**.
- Select a leaf image from your device.

## 3. Preview
- The image will appear in the preview area.

## 4. Predict Disease
- Click **Predict Disease**.
- The system will display:
  - **Prediction**: the detected disease
  - **Suggestion**: actions to prevent further damage

## 5. Logout
- Close the browser or refresh to log out (future versions may have a logout button).

## Notes
- Supported image types: `.jpg`, `.png`, `.jpeg`
- For best results, use clear images of individual leaves.
- Currently, the prediction is simulated (placeholder) – real predictions will require backend integration.
